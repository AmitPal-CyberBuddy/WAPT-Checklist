OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Safe by default.
Rigorous by design.

  • 01
    Context before coverage

    Unknown answers widen the plan. Known exclusions reduce noise without deleting your recorded work.

  • 02
    Evidence before severity

    A scanner lead, absent header, or theoretical path is not a finding until behavior and impact are demonstrated.

  • 03
    Do no harm

    Disruptive methods carry stop conditions. Destructive and denial-of-service material remains review-only.
