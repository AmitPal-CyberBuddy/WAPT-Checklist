ONE ITEM / ONE OBJECTIVE

Decision-grade test design

Every production item is a repeatable decision procedure—not a vulnerability name or payload list.

Objective

Controlled method

What changes, what stays constant, and where must testing stop?

Decision boundary

What observable behavior is secure, vulnerable, blocked, or inconclusive?

False positives

Evidence

Authorized only

Unknown stays visible

Uncertain scope produces Confirm—not silent exclusion or a guessed fact.

Scanner ≠ finding

Missing headers, signatures, timing, and tool alerts require behavior and impact.

Local by design
